How it works

1

Add your date

Drop in your anniversary and we start the clock. You'll hear from us at 30, 7, and 1 day out, so it never sneaks up on you — whether or not you ever book a plan with us.

2

Tell us what they love

A quick form: their taste, a budget range, anything you already have in mind. Takes about two minutes, and the more specific you are, the better the plan.

3

We put a plan together

We research real options in your area — a reservation or package, an activity, a gift idea — matched to the taste and budget you gave us. You'll see categories and a vibe first, not locked-in vendor names, so there's a plan to react to before anything's final.

4

Review and approve one invoice

Once you're happy with the direction, we send a single itemized invoice: the base fee, each booking's real cost, and our service fee, all spelled out line by line. Nothing is booked, and no vendor is paid, until you approve it.

5

We book it, you show up

After payment, we reserve, purchase, and confirm everything on the invoice — and handle it if a vendor cancels or something needs to change. You just show up.

What it costs

Reminders Free, always
Base planning fee $79 flat
Service fee 10% of what we book

The 10% only applies to reservations, activities, and gifts we actually book and pay for on your behalf — never an open-ended dinner tab. You see the full breakdown on one invoice before anything's booked or charged.
